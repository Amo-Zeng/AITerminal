# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Project participants must communicate respectfully, focus criticism on technical work, accept constructive feedback, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or disclosure of another person's private information.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful to the project or its community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.
